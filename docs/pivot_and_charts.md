# PivotTables and PivotCharts

## PivotTable views

### Company
Rows: Company  
Values: Average Price_euros

### Price Band
Rows: PriceBand  
Values: Count of Product

### Laptop Type
Rows: TypeName  
Values: Average Price_euros

### RAM Band
Rows: RAM_Band  
Values: Average Price_euros

## Dashboard charts

1. Average Laptop Price by Company
2. Average Laptop Price by Type
3. Laptop Mix by Price Band
4. Average Laptop Price by RAM

A helper RAM sort field was used during analysis. The final RAM chart on the dashboard was rebuilt from a clean RAM-band summary so helper numbers are not displayed as categories.
